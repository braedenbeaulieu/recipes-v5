## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds boneless chicken thighs, sliced
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper
- Tortillas and lime wedges, for serving

## Tools

- Large sheet pan
- Mixing bowl
- Chef's knife
- Tongs

## Directions

### Season everything

Toss the chicken, peppers, and onion with olive oil and all spices until evenly coated.

### Roast

Spread everything on a sheet pan and roast at 425°F until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are browned at the edges, about 18 to 20 minutes.

### Serve

Warm the tortillas, then fill with the roasted fajita mixture. Finish with lime juice.

## Notes

- Broil for the last 2 minutes if you want more char.
- This also works well with shrimp; reduce the cooking time.
